# Payment vouchers

Open **Payment vouchers** in the navigation, or `/vouchers`. Access follows bank management: organization administrators and superadmins only.

Create a voucher with a voucher number (generated by default and editable before saving), payment date, linked bank account, payee, amount, and payment details. Category, bill/invoice reference, and notes are optional. Saving posts the voucher and deducts the bank balance in one transaction. Future payment dates, fractional cents, insufficient funds, archived accounts, and bank accounts from another organization are rejected.

Retries use the original request key. Concurrent submissions cannot deduct twice or overdraw the account. Voucher numbers are unique within an organization, including voided vouchers. Currency comes from the linked bank.

Posted payment details are retained for audit. Use the leftmost row action to view details or **Void and restore balance** with a reason and date. Voiding records a linked credit once while keeping the original voucher and debit. An archived account must first be restored before a voucher can be voided. Generic bank reversals cannot reverse voucher entries; the voucher action keeps status and ledger balances consistent.

Bank transaction history includes Voucher payment and Voucher void filters. The vouchers page supports search, bank/status/date filters, pagination, details and action history. A reference identifies another document but does not automatically settle debts, expenses or invoices. Use Cheque management for payments awaiting cheque clearance rather than recording the same payment again as a voucher.

The database harness is `api/scripts/verify-vouchers.js`; it requires an empty disposable `voucher_test` database on `voucher-test-mysql`, configured through `VOUCHER_TEST_HOST`, `VOUCHER_TEST_DATABASE`, and `VOUCHER_TEST_PASSWORD`. It refuses other targets or nonempty databases.
